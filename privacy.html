Wild Sprites YouTube Privacy Terms

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy covers "Officed", a private production app used only by the owner of the Wild Sprites YouTube channel, and this website.

Who uses the app

Officed is not offered to the public. Its only user is the owner of the Wild Sprites channel, who signs in with their own Google account to let the app upload videos to their own channel.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, Officed uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload the channel's own finished videos and schedule them,
  • set each video's title, description, tags, thumbnail and captions,
  • read the channel's name and ID to confirm it is uploading to the right channel.

It does not read, collect or store data about any other person, viewer, subscriber or channel.

How data is stored

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Officed's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

The owner can remove Officed's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and by deleting the stored token from their computer.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or forms, and does not collect personal information.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on the Wild Sprites YouTube channel.
